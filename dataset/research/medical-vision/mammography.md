# Four-view mammography assessment

## perc-mammo-deepmind

The work is a malignancy-suspicion score for one bilateral mammography examination, using four current CC/MLO views and patient age. It excludes image acquisition, a written clinical report, patient contact and later diagnostic work. The human baseline is one radiologist's assessment, not pooled judgments from six radiologists.

[McKinney et al. 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-019-1799-6) reports that the system exceeded all six readers and that its AUC exceeded the average reader AUC by 11.5 percentage points. This supports **above** for that reader comparison. [Extended Data Table 3](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-019-1799-6/tables/3) describes 500 selected cases enriched for cancer and challenging negatives; 465 remained after implant and image-quality exclusions. This is the reader-study case distribution, not routine screening prevalence. The observation is a score per exam, not an 88% reduction in reading work from the paper's separate simulation.

The [revised original supplement](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41586-020-2679-9/MediaObjects/41586_2020_2679_MOESM1_ESM.pdf) is the main compute evidence. It averages three stochastic training runs of each of three model branches:

| Branch | Reported workload per checkpoint | Reconstructed branch FLOPs, including all three checkpoints |
|---|---|---:|
| Case | Four 2048×2048 ResNet-v1-50 feature extractions, concatenation and a 512-unit hidden layer; 500 test augmentations | 3.4910e15 |
| Lesion | RetinaNet on four 2048×2048 images; ten selected lesions plus ten contralateral patches; MobileNetV2 on 409×409 patches, 500 augmented classifications | 8.0338e13 |
| Breast | Four 4096×3328 ResNet-v2-50 extractions, then twelve bottleneck blocks per breast | 2.3344e13 |

The central recipe totals **3.5947e15 FLOPs**, rounded to **3.6e15** in the CSV. It counts convolution and dense multiply-adds as two operations. It does not infer operations from TPU utilization or latency. `count_models.py`, `calculations.json` and `agent-work/sources/medical-vision/operation-ledger.json` retain the calculation. TensorFlow's original ResNet and MobileNet source files are retained beside the supplement.

The threefold checkpoint ensemble and 500-fold augmentation are separate factors. The lesion detector is reused across its second-stage augmentations, consistent with the fixed detector described in the supplement. Ten lesions means twenty image patches once the contralateral inputs are counted. The 409×409 patch size and 576×576 extraction come from the revised supplement; the earlier supplement used different crop wording.

Several implementation choices are assumptions. Use the original TensorFlow-Slim ResNet channel widths and downsampling convention, standard width-1 MobileNetV2, three replicated image channels, pooled feature vectors for the case head, and a standard 256-channel ResNet50/FPN RetinaNet with nine anchors. The breast head's channels are unreported; use 2,048 output channels and 512 bottleneck channels through its 4/2/2 downsampling groups. Its final projection produces one score. The lesion classification head is approximated by a linear binary classifier from two pooled patch features and metadata. These heads and detector account for little of the central total.

The breast branch's test-time repetition count is **not specified**, even in the revised supplement. The central recipe uses one breast evaluation per checkpoint because the supplement explicitly assigns 500 evaluations only to the other branches. If the breast branch also used 500, the same architecture assumptions give **1.5243e16 FLOPs**, 4.24 times the central estimate. This is the main unresolved compute limit. It is a sensitivity case, not a claimed upper bound. Using the original ResNet first-block stride convention for the case branch instead changes the central total to 3.9716e15. Flattening its spatial features rather than pooling changes it to 3.6462e15. The missing breast repetition count cannot be resolved from the paper; the central estimate should remain visibly assumption-based.

The source gives no timed reading study. [Haygood et al. 2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4124527/), Table 3, reports **167 seconds** for four-view digital mammograms. Its overall 240-second mean includes many exams with more than four views, and is unsuitable here. Four radiologists were timed while interpreting and entering reports in clinical practice. The paper also reports approximately **11 seconds** of mechanical transition between cases. The table gives marginal comparisons, so the 167-second four-view value cannot be combined with the separate no-prior-study mean as though they were disjoint components. The analyzed cohort excludes aborted readings and includes only completed reports; use subset successful for that completion criterion, not diagnostic correctness. Human_attempts remains blank: the paper gives 268 analyzed digital examinations overall but not the number of four-view readings, and the separately cited case-transition timing sample is also unrecovered. Four radiologists are not four timing attempts.

Estimate **150 active seconds** for the image-based suspicion judgment: 167 minus the roughly 11-second case transition gives 156, rounded to 150. This transfers a clinical reading duration to the experimental decision. Clinical reporting and any prior comparisons make the timing source broader, while the reader study's deliberately difficult case mix can require more scrutiny. These effects are not individually timed, so the central adjustment is deliberately small. The bounds come from Haygood's own between-reader spread: the four radiologists' mean digital interpretation times run 171 to 300 seconds against the 240-second all-reader mean, 0.71× to 1.25×. Applied to the four-view figure and less the 11-second transition, that is **108 to 198 seconds**. It is a spread of reader means, not a confidence interval. The timing transfer remains distinct from the performance protocol: revised Supplementary Figures 4 and 5 show that performance readers also supplied lesion boxes and BI-RADS ratings. Those extra reader outputs justify different_task; the separate timing population justifies different_human_baseline. It must not present 150 seconds as measured experimental image-only reading time.

The model organization is Google Health / DeepMind. Exact public availability of the research weights is not established. The authors say their training code depends on internal tooling and cannot be released; the paper date is not entered as a model release date.
