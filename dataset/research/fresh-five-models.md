# Model assumptions established for this collection

The parameter coefficient is 2×active parameters and omits attention. All closed-model values are estimates, not disclosed architectures.

- **latxa-70b-v1.1:** the fresh original-source reconstruction now uses 68,976,648,192 architecture-derived parameters and the February 17, 2024 complete-weight commit as the best release-date evidence. Historical repository visibility is not independently verified. See [the current study record](latxa/latxa.md#release-dates).
- **gpt-4o-2024-08-06:** 50B active, coefficient 1e11, deriving a central estimate from [Epoch AI's energy analysis](https://epoch.ai/gradient-updates/how-much-energy-does-chatgpt-use). The article gives a central total estimate of 200B (100–400B), but deliberately takes the pessimistic 400B endpoint for its energy calculation. I retain its assumed one-quarter activation while using the central total: 200B/4=50B active. The source's 100B active energy case is therefore a high sensitivity case, not our best estimate. Neither total size nor activation ratio is an OpenAI disclosure. [OpenAI's Structured Outputs release](https://openai.com/index/introducing-structured-outputs-in-the-api/) establishes the revision's August 6, 2024 availability. Vision operation assumptions are separate in imagenet.md; models.csv records the estimated 2B encoder, rounded from the approximately 1.84B vision backbone of OpenCLIP ViT-bigG/14.
- **gpt-5-high:** 100B active, coefficient 2e11, adopting Epoch's [original GPT-5 compute analysis](https://epochai.substack.com/p/notes-on-gpt-5-training-compute), which places active size near 100B based on speed, pricing and comparable models. [OpenAI system card](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-system-card/) is dated August 7, 2025. This is the reasoning model under high effort, not the ChatGPT router plus a mixture of unrelated requests.
- **o3-codeforces-checkpoint:** 50B active, coefficient 1e11, an explicit transfer of the central GPT-4o-scale frontier architecture estimate, not a reported o3 size. [Competitive Programming with Large Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06807v1), section 4, distinguishes the early Codeforces checkpoint from the later IOI checkpoint and public o3. No supported public release date exists for the exact checkpoint. Retain blank. This size has substantially weaker evidence than the other two closed-model estimates: a 25–100B range would change compute by ×0.5–2 without changing any task workload. There is no independent architectural evidence in the inspected sources for retaining the earlier 100B transfer after correcting its GPT-4o basis. Accordingly both o3 generation and the unidentified ranking model receive the revised 50B assumption and 1e11 coefficient. This is a correlated modeling assumption, not evidence that their architectures are actually equal.

The Latxa registry entry was subsequently updated from a fresh original-source reconstruction; its before-values are retained outside the dataset. No model's API dollar price is treated as a direct FLOP measurement.
