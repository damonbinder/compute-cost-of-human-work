# Original Arabic MMLU corruption audit

The retained source test CSVs contain 1,489 near-duplicate questions asking how to detect IP spoofing, spread across 50 subjects. This is 10.604% of 14,042 questions and 9.50184% of the paper's nested score weight. Initial substring screening gave 1,494; five unrelated hits (leadership, MISSISSIPPI, and three actual network-protocol questions) were inspected and excluded. The remaining family includes 1,465 items with both TTL and TCP distractors and 24 spelling/translation variants. It may include an original genuine cybersecurity question, so the count describes the repeated family, not proof that every individual member was incorrectly translated. Its appearance throughout anatomy, history, medicine and law, with the same options in the same order but A/B/C/D keys, is clear dataset corruption.

Keys are A383/B317/C407/D382. Examples in the JSON include professional-law questions with essentially identical spoofing queries/options but conflicting correct letters. The selected native model records match their source CSV query, options and key exactly. The corruption precedes model evaluation; it is not introduced by our extraction.

In the six retained paired 7B subjects, 51 of 1,279 rows belong to the family. Family correctness changes16→18; remaining correctness 342→391. Most observed net gain in this limited subset is outside the family. The six subjects contribute 9.375% of the complete nested score; do not extrapolate these counts into a cleaned score for all 57 subjects.

The source's weighted score remains a usable but noisy capability endpoint. Human study should improve interpretation and recall on the meaningful questions, not learn contradictory keys. At an illustrative 25% expected accuracy on the defective family, the 7B published 29.47→32.14% transition corresponds to29.94→32.88% on the remaining score weight. The 13B33.76→40.45% transition corresponds to34.68→42.08%. This is a task-composition diagnostic, not a measured accuracy correction or hours-per-point conversion.

The 7B 60-hour central remains a reasonable broad assumed route with basic Arabic and subject prerequisites, since the required gain on meaningful material remains modest and the uncertainty range was already wide. Its prose must explicitly call this benchmark performance rather than clean Arabic language acquisition. A separate authoring patch recommends this narrative correction; numerical estimates are unchanged.

Reproduce using `audit_corruption.py --shared-sources /path/to/sources/acegpt-learning --output /path/to/new-audit.json`. This reads the accepted study's original source files and writes only to a new path outside them. All flagged locators and five excluded legitimate substring hits are retained for review. The audit is not an exhaustive search for every kind of translation error.
