Compute cost of human work

About

Each point is a task, with an estimate of how many FLOPs an AI system needs to do it and how long it takes a person to do it. Where available, the dollar price for the AI to do it is also included. The estimates are very crude, and the uncertainty toggle shows a plausible range for the relevant values.

The cheapest-per-task toggle shows, for each task, only the model that completed it at human level or better for the fewest FLOPs.

Further information is in the write-up and the full dataset on GitHub.
